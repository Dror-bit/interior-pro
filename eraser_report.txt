REPORT - the eraser, and the dimension locking onto corners  (2026-08-19)

== test run ==
129 suites green (81 ruby + 48 js). 0 failed. New: t51. Updated: rt65.

== what he showed me ==
Two SketchUp screenshots: the eraser passing over a dimension, the dimension
lit BLUE, and an eraser icon for a cursor.
And: "אני דוקר שתי נקודות שאגב הלחיצה צריכה להינעל על פינות וקצוות כמו בשאר
הכלים".

== what was actually wrong ==
The eraser rubbed out free SITE geometry and NOTHING else. A dimension could
only be removed by picking it and pressing Delete - and once one-click label
picking went in, a click that landed on a label selected the label instead of
the mark, so Delete did nothing. That is the "אני גם לא יכול למחוק את המידות".
Fixed at the root: the eraser now takes dimensions and notes, which is how he
was trying to do it in the first place.

== what changed - plan_sheet_dialog.rb only ==
hitErase()      marks first, then site lines. Marks win: they are the small
                thing drawn on top and they are what he is aiming at.
ERA             now {kind:'mark'|'site', i:index} instead of a bare index
paintErase()    lights up everything the sweep has crossed, in BLUE (#1f6feb)
                to match the picture he sent - "the eraser is on it", not
                "it is already gone". Still one node, still never calls
                render(): a real back yard is thousands of lines.
ERASWEEP        press, drag across a run of things, let go and they all go -
                the SketchUp behaviour. One trip to Ruby for the site lines,
                not one per line.
eraseSweep()    marks removed highest-index-first so taking one out cannot
                move the next; the same in Ruby for site lines (.sort.reverse)
eraserCursor()  the eraser icon itself, inline SVG - he asked for the cursor
                by name. A missing icon FILE would have failed silently to an
                arrow, so it is drawn in code.
drop_site_line  now also accepts {is:[...]}, a whole sweep at once

snapPoint()     THE OTHER REQUEST. A click while drawing a dimension locks
                onto the nearest CORNER: every end of a line and every vertex
                of a polyline/polygon on a visible layer, plus the ends of
                dimensions already drawn so a run of them lines up.
                Measured in SCREEN PIXELS (12), not model inches, so the pull
                feels the same at any zoom - the SketchUp behaviour.
                A hidden layer offers nothing: what he cannot see, he cannot
                snap to.
paintSnap()     a green square on the corner it will lock to, shown BEFORE he
                clicks - a snap he cannot see is a snap he has to trust.
